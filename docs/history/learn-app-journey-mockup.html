why can it be gamed if humans ranked the data?

TATA · thought about it · 1m at 3:01:00👍

Because the reward model is not the humans, it is a network trained to imitate their rankings on a finite set. Optimise hard against any imitation and you find inputs where it is wrong: strings that score high with the reward model and mean nothing to a person. Karpathy shows “the the the” scoring as a top answer. That is why RLHF runs for a limited number of steps and then stops.

The brain updates itself

At ten every night, a launchd job — the same shape as antifeed’s and fiveaside’s — asks the desk which videos have new notes, regenerates each one’s post, commits it to the brain repo and deploys. If nothing changed, it does nothing. You never run a command. One post per video, all sittings merged, key concepts in your words on top, the margin below, widgets as stills.

Decided: the job is margin’s. com.kb.margin lives in the margin repo and is the only thing that moves; the brain stays dumb — a static site that runs nothing and knows nothing about margin. margin writes into the brain’s posts/ files it owns plus their index rows, commits, deploys. The brain’s own rule (“nothing posted or deployed unless KB asks”) gets one documented exception, named in brain/AGENTS.md: margin’s resource posts.

What is under it

The desk, and how margin talks to it

margin is two halves. The sidebar is the extension in Zen: what you see and type. The desk is a small server on Cloudflare — margin-3d0.pages.dev — that the sidebar talks to. It is the TA’s desk: where the TA sits, and where the notebook is kept. It has three jobs and no front page.

Holds the keyThe OpenRouter key lives only on the desk. The sidebar never has it, so nothing in your browser can leak it. Every reply is: sidebar → desk → GLM → desk → sidebar. Keeps the notebookEvery note, reply, ack and star is stored on the desk (Cloudflare KV). That is why any Firefox profile or machine shows the same threads, why a reinstall loses nothing, and why the nightly job can read it all without your laptop being on. Serves the static bitsThe brain notes, the transcripts, and the sandboxed page a widget runs in. The sidebar fetches them; they never ship inside the extension.

How they connect. Once, in the gear: the desk’s address and a token you made up. After that, every time you press Enter the sidebar sends the note to the desk (PUT /api/notes) and asks for a reply (POST /api/ask); on opening a video it pulls the threads down (GET /api/notes). All over HTTPS with the token in a header; the desk refuses anything without it. The sidebar also keeps a local copy, so it renders instantly and works with the desk down — the desk is the truth, the copy is the cache.

What the desk does when asked. It finds the two or three brain notes closest to your note — by words, and by where you are in the lecture — hands them to GLM with a strict shape to fill in, checks the shape (one concept, under 170 words, a real timestamp, no numbers that are not in the notes), and stores the reply. If GLM says the notes were not enough, the desk sends the same question to the bigger model with the transcript, and you see that reply instead. If you asked to be shown something, it builds the widget. None of that is in the sidebar; the sidebar only shows what comes back.

The nightly job is a third piece, on your laptop: com.kb.margin asks the desk which videos changed, renders their posts, commits them to the brain and deploys. The desk does not know the brain exists; the brain does not know margin exists.

What is left

  • One box. Type, Enter. Questions answered, statements checked.
  • Threads. Reply under any answer; the TA thinks harder on its own when it has to.
  • Ask to be shown in a thread and the widget is the reply.
  • 👍 on any reply to say it needs nothing more; ☆ on anything to keep it; Keep my version on a correction.
  • The brain post, regenerated nightly, one per video.
  • Settings, once. The gear.

What is gone

  • Sessions: start, end, break, digest, export.
  • Tags, the Ask checkbox, Doubt/Note buttons.
  • "Not convinced", "Poke at it", "go deeper on all", timestamp chips.
  • The tracker coupling. The tracker is the plan; margin is the notebook; the brain is the record.
  • Every terminal command after install.
  • The milestone chip; the "Check this" imperative.
